So, let's talk about how TPM works. There are two keys that matter - the Endorsement Key, or EK, and the Attestation Key, or AK. The EK is like a unique identifier for the TPM chip itself. It's burned into the chip by the manufacturer, and it's used to verify that the chip is genuine. The AK, on the other hand, is a signing key that's generated inside the TPM, under the EK's hierarchy.
